# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the
BLEhound community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
project maintainers via the repository's issue tracker or by email to
woowill007@gmail.com.
Maintainers will review and respond to reports and may remove, edit, or reject
contributions, comments, and contributors that violate this Code of Conduct.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
