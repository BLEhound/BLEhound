BLEhound case design

BLEhound · 3D-printed case · Variant A revised

Tray + lid, screws enter from the bottom, only LEDs on the top face

Changed per your three requirements: M3 countersunk from the bottom, only 4 LED holes on the top, inner cavity sized for the soldered pin-header height. During review I found 7 more things to fix; all are now in the drawings and the OpenSCAD source.

Recomputed against the connector spec

SMA
SMA-KWE external-thread inner-hole right-angle jack: overall 14.5, thread 8.5, body 6 × 6 × 9.2, pin 3.5, pin pitch 5.1 × 5.1 (matches the board’s 5.15 pads).From the drawing: the Ø6.35 thread top is flush with the body top, soaxis is 9.2 − 3.2 = 6.0 above the board face, not the 3.2 I earlier estimated for an ordinary right-angle jack. Body front face is 0.4 beyond the front pin row → 1.0 inside the board edge; thread extends past the board edge 7.5. Fix:move the parting line from 7.0 to 6.0 (= axis), the three Ø7.4 holes are half-circle top and bottom, the board drops in flat and closes when the lid is on, sturdier than a U-slot; the 9.2-tall body leaves 0.8 in the 10.0 cavity.
Thread
Usable thread past the wall 5.0:8.5 − 1.0 inside board − 0.5 clearance − 2.0 wall. The nut that comes with the jack is 2.2 thick; after tightening, 2.8 is left for the antenna’s inner nut (about 4 turns, enough). No nut also works — the hole already clamps the thread top and bottom and the screw locks the board; the nut only stiffens the wall.
USB-C
Cutout reduced from 13 × 7 to 9.8 × 4.0.You measured the receptacle overhang past the board edge as 1.4, so the receptacle front face is only 2.5 − 1.4 = 1.1; the plug’s metal shell (8.3 × 2.6, length 6.5) fully enters the receptacle, the overmold stays outside the wall, and the cutout only needs to clear the receptacle body (8.94 × 3.26 + 0.4 per side). Add a 0.8 45° lead-in chamfer on the outside. There is still 2.4 of wall from the cutout top to the parting line — no longer a thin bridge.
Antenna
2.4G stub 5 cm right-angle antenna (SMA inner-thread inner-pin): the elbow in any direction clears the neighboring jack — the three holes are 23.2 apart and the elbow body is about 9 in diameter, just enough.

Review conclusion: 10 changes

The first three are your requirements; the rest are issues found and resolved while checking the original Variant A item by item.

Requirement
Screws moved to the bottom.M3×12 flat-head countersunk from the tray bottom, through the Ø8 bosses and the PCB mounting holes, into M3 heat-set inserts at the lower end of the lid bosses. The top is clean; the foot pads are moved inward, clear of the screws.
Requirement
Only LEDs on the top face.4 Ø2.2 holes (CH37/38/39/PWR) + engraved text. Buttons and SWD require opening the lid; the source keeps a pin_holes parameter, off by default.
Requirement
Leave room for the soldered pin headers.8.5 above the pin header + 1.5 margin → cavity clear height 10.0; pin tails + solder joints below the board → standoff 4.0. Total height 19.6.
Issue 1
The SMA hole cannot be a full circle in the tray wall.With the SMA-KWE axis at 6.0, the Ø7.4 hole spans 2.3–9.7 and necessarily crosses the parting line. So the parting line is placed on the axis: lower half in the tray, upper half in the lid; the board drops in flat and closes on assembly.
Issue 2
The USB-C receptacle overhangs the board edge by 1.4 and enters the rear wall by 0.9; the board cannot drop straight into a closed cutout.Assembly changes to: insert the USB end into the cutout at an angle first (only 0.9 into the wall, a 5° tilt is enough), then lower the antenna end so the SMA thread drops into the lower half-circle. To avoid the tilt, open the cutout down to the parting line (it becomes 9.8 × 6.4).
Issue 3
An M3 countersink in the 2 mm floor leaves only 0.3 mm of material.The countersink sits directly under the Ø8 boss, so the actual material is floor 2 + boss 4; a 1.5 countersink is fine. But the foot pads can no longer sit over the screws, so they move to (12,10)(58,10)(12,41)(58,41).
Issue 4
A Ø7 boss with an M3 heat-set insert (OD 4.6) has only 1.2 wall.Boss changed to Ø8. Checked components within 7.5 mm of the four holes against the coordinate file: the nearest is C46 (0402) beside H1, 1.3 mm from the boss edge; the other three holes are clear.
Issue 5
The LED is 9 mm below the lid; the Ø2 hole is visible but dim, and the three channels bleed light.Add a Ø4.6 light pipe under the lid, down to z=2.5 (LED height 0.7, adjacent button height 1.5, both clear). Brighter option: change the hole to Ø1.85 and insert a 12 mm clear 1.75 filament as a light guide.
Issue 6
Board corners vs. cavity fillets.The PCB has square corners; cavity corner R1 with 0.5 per-side clearance, checked non-interfering (0.3 at the corner). The three ANT2 reserved positions are not slotted by default; to populate them set ant2_slots=true and reprint the tray.
Issue 7
Screw length.Floor 2 + standoff 4 + board 1.6 = 7.6; M3×12 goes 4.4 mm into the insert (insert length 5, hole depth 6.5 for the screw tip). M3×14 also works, don’t exceed 14.

Hardware BOM and assembly

Hardware

  • M3×12 flat-head (countersunk) screw ×4, stainless or black zinc
  • M3 heat-set insert ×4, M3×4×5 (OD 4.6), pilot hole Ø4.0 (some batches 4.2)
  • Ø8 × 1.5 silicone foot pad ×4
  • SMA nut (2.2 thick) + spring washer: comes with the connector, optional

Assembly order

  • Press the heat-set inserts into the lower end of the 4 lid bosses (iron 220 ℃, flush with the boss face)
  • Place the PCB into the tray: USB end first, angling the USB-C receptacle into the cutout, then lower the antenna end so the 3 SMA threads drop into the lower half-circle
  • Close the lid, mate the upper half-circle, seat the inner lip into the tray, align the bosses with the board holes
  • Flip the whole thing, drive the 4 M3×12 from the bottom; stick on the foot pads
  • Fit the SMA nuts (lock outside the wall, pressing the two shells together) and the antenna

Printing

  • Tray: opening up, no supports. USB cutout bridges 9.8 mm; SMA half-circle slots open upward
  • Lid: in the source part="lid" is pre-flipped, top face on the bed; bosses and light pipes point up; the three half-circles on the skirt open upward, no supports
  • PETG or PLA, layer 0.2, 3 walls, 4 top/bottom layers, 20% infill
  • Bosses and light pipes with diameter ≥ 4.6 print stably; the countersink is a 90° cone, no supports needed

Numbers still for you to check

  1. SMA body height.Measured 9.2 from the spec drawing (axis 6.0); after soldering, caliper the thread center to the board face — 6.3 is also fine (the Ø7.4 hole leaves ±0.5).
  2. USB-C receptacle overhang 1.4 changed as you said.Any actual value between 1.0–1.8 works; the cutout needs no further change.
  3. Pin-header type.Computed for a standard 2.54 header: top 6 + body 2.5 = 8.5, tail 3.0 below (1.4 exposed below the board + solder). If you use a taller header, change inner_h.
